SceneGIS modifications (2026): adapted Lanczos current-color reconstruction,
five-tap Catmull-Rom history sampling with deringing, neighborhood/variance bounds,
segment clipping and HDR blending for Cesium GLSL. Added linear-depth reprojection,
CPU camera-relative transforms, silhouette support, depth rejection and current alpha.
No Filament renderer, material compiler or runtime is included.

Original history-filter source credits retained from Filament:
M. J. P. (https://therealmjp.github.io/),
http://vec3.ca/bicubic-filtering-in-fewer-taps/,
Jorge Jimenez, SIGGRAPH 2016, Filmic SMAA (five-tap optimization).
Variance clipping: Marco Salvi, An Excursion in Temporal Supersampling.
