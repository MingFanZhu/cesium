## Remaining risks / handoff

Enclosed mode is 6-connected grid cavity fill, not proof of manifold solidity. Surface shells may thicken thin geometry; clipped or open surfaces may leak, coarse grids may seal holes. V2 must preserve this distinction and add explicit source completeness/LOD semantics before the 3D Tiles adapter is considered delivered.

V1 is ready for its local phase checkpoint; V2 remains pending until that commit exists.
