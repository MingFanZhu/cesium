## Source facts and implementation

FFT finalize already computes the horizontal displacement Jacobian. Its normal
alpha is instantaneous foam. Retain this source, exclude the fine ripple band
from whitecap generation, and accumulate decaying coverage in a second normal
texture per band. Alternate framebuffer attachments while sharing displacement;
there is no additional draw or CPU readback. Advect history with a small explicit
wind drift. Scene time controls decay; frozen time reuses output, rewind or wave
configuration changes invalidate history. `foamHalfLife=0` retains instantaneous
coverage. Destruction releases both normal textures and the shared displacement.

Shade crest coverage with filtered bubble openings and macro breakup. Keep the
existing scene-depth contact source for shallow foam, with smoother coverage;
do not claim bathymetry-based breakers or transported shoreline foam. A stable
bathymetry input remains follow-up work rather than an invented terrain query.

Replace preset-only authoring with editable transmission color, reference distance,
scattering color and concentration. Convert picker sRGB to scene linear, then
derive Beer absorption and RGB scattering. Presets only fill these controls;
legacy mode remains selectable. This adapter belongs to the example, while the
engine retains its coefficient API and unrestricted color support.
