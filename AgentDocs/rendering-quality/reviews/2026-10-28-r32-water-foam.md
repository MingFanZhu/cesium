# R32 technical review

- Type: review; author/owner: current architecture and execution task; 2026-10-28.
- Target: [R32](../plans/2026-10-28-r32-water-foam.md), ADR-0195.
- Status: technical checkpoint; owner visual acceptance remains open.

## Findings and evidence

- Fixed: crest coverage used to disappear immediately after compression. GPU tests
  verify one/two half-life decay, wind advection, pause, rewind, zero half-life and
  destruction of both history textures. Seven wind tests pass in development and release.
- Fixed: presets prevented color authoring. Both-world browser tests edit picker
  values and verify the resulting absorption/scattering, then exercise four presets,
  both wave modes, camera movement and disposal. Two browser cases pass; screenshots
  inspected. Water/optics development GPU suite: 22 pass.
- Resource review: shared displacement is owned only by primary framebuffer;
  alternate framebuffer borrows it and its separate normal is explicitly destroyed.
  No additional FFT draw; three 128-square mipmapped RGBA16F histories add 0.5 MiB.
- Builds: development, release, generated declarations and tsc pass. JavaScript
  lint and upstream audit pass; no upstream-owned or generated delivery files.
- Bounded RTX 3060 Ti / Chrome 156 / 1185x800 full-frame samples are 2.91–4.25 ms
  for the frozen Planar fixture. These include all scene work and are not an
  isolated animated-foam cost or a demonstrated speedup. Added shader work is one
  history read per FFT texel plus filtered crest detail at surface pixels.
- Open baseline: RiverSurface Globe phase-wrap test at line 550 fails its <=1
  byte per-pixel continuity threshold. Replacing all three changed engine sources
  with HEAD and rerunning reproduces the same failure. Other ten river cases pass.
  Keep the baseline finding; do not weaken its threshold or hide the failure.
- Deferred scope: bathymetry-based shoreline transport is not implemented. Contact
  foam still depends on opaque screen depth. User visual quality judgment remains
  separate; R25 cold-mesh and R27 earlier findings remain open.

R32 is eligible for its independent local checkpoint. R33 may activate after that
commit under the owner's current request to continue underwater improvements.
