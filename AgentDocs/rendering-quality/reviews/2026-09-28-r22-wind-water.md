## Delivered behavior

Optional caller-owned SceneGISWindWaves drives existing SceneGISWaterSurface, with the
same local XY / rigid modelMatrix contract in Globe and Planar. Seeded JONSWAP/TMA bands
use finite-depth dispersion, MRT float inverse FFT, displacement, normals and crest foam.
Wind speed/travel direction, fetch, mean depth and artistic scale are editable. Hidden
surfaces do not advance it; identical Scene time and settings reuse outputs. Sharing one
instance within one Context avoids duplicate FFT work. The caller removes borrowing
surfaces and destroys the simulation before destroying the Viewer/Context.

128 m clipped mesh tiles preserve concave outlines and island holes. Interior LOD cells
use two triangles, stitched boundaries use center fans and identical finest edge vertices.
Displacement is filtered by mesh spacing and pixel footprint. Culling includes the full
spectral displacement bound. Each tile keeps at most two LODs and inactive mesh storage
has a 16 MiB per-surface budget. Surface destruction releases retained geometry.

The Xuanwu Lake page contains local OSM relation 2138994 shoreline data, seven islands,
near/shore/overview cameras, wind presets, physical parameter controls, three simulation
qualities and pause. Bottom/landscape are explicitly illustrative, with ODbL attribution.
Water height is above the underlying World surface; no runtime public data dependency.
The existing PBR page and its user settings are untouched.

## Findings resolved

1. Native Texture defaults flip typed-array rows: explicitly disable Y flipping for FFT
   uploads. The independent CPU DFT exposed the mismatch and now agrees within half-float tolerance.
2. Physical-light shader rebuild initially omitted wind VS defines: preserve the defines
   in every program variant. Both-world lighting transitions exercise the correction.
3. A displaced surface can reflect an opaque submerged bottom: wind-water ray hits below
   its local mean plane are rejected. General SSR and legacy water variants are unchanged.
4. Camera pitch below the Planar controller's qualified range triggered its initial home
   view. Authored near cameras now meet the existing controller range; no navigation code changed.
5. Test cleanup originally destroyed the Context before caller-owned FFT shaders. Tests and
   examples now honor the public ownership order; no defensive product fallback was added.

## Remaining limitations

- Periodic, band-limited wind-wave synthesis with uniform mean depth and user-specified
  effective fetch. It does not solve along-shore wave refraction/diffraction, river current,
  bathymetry, wakes, wetting/drying, breaking-wave spray or hydraulic forecasts.
- Parameter edits immediately regenerate the spectrum; gradual weather transitions are
  not simulated. Extreme artistic amplitudes can fold the surface; normal lake settings
  are the visual target. Crest foam is an instantaneous compression approximation.
- Local ENU remains a plane in Globe; this is a kilometer-scale lake example, not a global ocean.
- Existing screen-space reflection/transmission and above-water limitations remain.
  Offscreen water may still update FFT while show=true; show=false and frozen time skip work.
- TAA has no object motion vectors for the animated surface; the example uses MSAA.
- New LOD geometry is generated synchronously on first use; rapid travel into uncached
  detailed tiles can have transient CPU cost. Stable views reuse the meshes.
- GPU budgets vary with device, water coverage, visible geometry and reflection settings.
