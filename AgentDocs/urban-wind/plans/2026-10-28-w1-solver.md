## Source facts and design

Owned Core validates units/MRT basis; owned Scene solver uses native persistent Texture,
Framebuffer and viewport DrawCommand like existing particles/PBF. GLSL under approved Wind root.
No new Scene hook. Public producer advances once per normal frame; pick/shadow/consumers cannot step.
Cell centers are minimum+(i+0.5)dx. Z slices tile in a 2D atlas; five RGBA32F distributions in each
of two buffers store f-w. Two fused pull/collision draws (4+1 outputs) and one macro-field draw.
Appendix A MRT, conserved rates zero, shear locally relaxed. W1 periodic faces; W2 owns walls/open faces.

Public SceneGISWindSimulation snapshots dimensions/cellSize/timeStep/kinematicViscosity,
smagorinskyConstant, initialVelocity, solidMask and placement. Budget/paused are mutable;
step/reset and completed simulationTime are explicit. Scene-time debt does not change physical dt.
getGpuField returns a live borrowed descriptor, never calls update.

## Steps and verification

1. Basis orthogonality/inverse, conserved collision moments, units and rejection tests.
2. GPU initialize/pull-MRT/macro; compare independent CPU collision for a homogeneous cell.
3. Uniform periodic flow and analytic shear decay, including two resolutions.
4. Fixed scheduling, duplicate frame/pick, pause/single-step/reset and destruction.
5. 128×128×32 memory and hardware GPU step timer; scoped Specs, release and public types.
6. Review and W1 checkpoint before W2.

## Results and risks

Core 3/3 and strict GPU 4/4 passed. Release/buildTs/tsc passed. Hardware median 0.365568 ms/step
at 128×128×32, three draws, 106168320 texture bytes. Details and limits in review.
No CPU fallback or generated-file edits. W1 does not claim urban/wall accuracy.
Tiny air viscosity remains a numerical risk; checkpoint precedes W2 activation.
