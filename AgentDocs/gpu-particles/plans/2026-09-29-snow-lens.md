Reuse SceneGISSnowLensStage and the existing example, replace flakeSize/meltDuration with coverage [0,1] (default 0.4), and retain intensity/enabled/Scene ownership. No legacy option aliases. A packaged generated grayscale crystal texture plus a soft irregular edge mask supplies static frost; intensity controls opacity and coverage its inward extent. No time, motion, impacts, simulation or fixed specular glints. The center remains clear at default coverage.

Owner requests a snow counterpart to the completed rain lens. Keep rain unchanged, including the rejected fixed-highlight removal. Existing native PostProcessStage supports a disabled-by-default, color-only stage with Scene-time animation and collection-owned destruction. No depth, history, collision, extra geometry or external asset is required for soft irregular snow flecks.

Add SceneGISSnowLensStage with scene/name/enabled, intensity [0,1], flakeSize [8,128] drawing-buffer pixels and meltDuration [1,30] seconds.
