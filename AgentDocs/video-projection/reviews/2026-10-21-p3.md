## Findings

  invalidate its previously scheduled frame callback, not only replacing the
  element. Source reset now cancels / reschedules with a new generation. Resize
  invalidates readiness. A real stream replacement Spec verifies dimensions,
  released old texture and rejection of an obsolete callback.
- Fixed: initial async media playback must not delay installing pagehide cleanup.
  The example starts media asynchronously, installs ownership cleanup immediately,
  then waits before assembling the scene. BFCache keeps the live page intact.
- Accepted boundary: transparent foreground can be overlaid at opaque receiver
  pixels. Public documentation and GPU Spec explicitly record this display-stage
  contract. Optical transparency remains outside the first version.
- No blocking findings. Only self-review was used. Final visual judgement belongs
  to the owner; it is not inferred from passing pixel counters.

Capture CPU is the sum for enabled projectors; latest upload CPU samples were
about 1.0–1.1 ms (720p) and 1.8–1.9 ms (1080p). Video byte estimates exclude decoder
and post-process targets; each 1024 depth target adds about 4 MiB. Native timer
queries measure the whole scene frame, not isolated projection work. Short GPU
samples include warm-state / scheduling variation (disabled sample was slower
than some enabled samples), so they do not establish a causal incremental GPU cost.
Do not claim a speedup or general frame budget from this table. Upload counters,
fixed per-observer draws and owned texture sizes directly establish bounded work.
