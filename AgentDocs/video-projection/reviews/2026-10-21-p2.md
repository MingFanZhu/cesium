## Findings and evidence

- Resolved: raw native video uniforms upload only when dirty. Owned VideoTexture
  uses native frame notifications, uploads during update and cancels obsolete
  callbacks. Real decoded stream test confirms one new frame causes one upload;
  five additional renders do not upload. Removal preserves caller-owned tracks.
- Resolved: display-stage transparency must be explicit. Product documentation
  and GPU test record transparent-foreground overlay, not physical transmission.
- Resolved: SDR video must bypass scene exposure. Both Worlds verify red / green /
  blue / 128 gray in HDR and LDR, aspect bars, flipY, opacity and black content.
- No blocking findings: geometry / World checks reuse ObserverState, missing
  media suppresses overlay, errors stop notifications, dimensions rebuild texture,
  and source replacement / seek invalidate readiness. Paused video remains fixed
  while moving offscreen occluders changes visibility. Viewshed and shadows coexist.

Seven GPU Specs pass; full build, build-ts and tsc pass. Generated declaration
exports the class and its typed media / status / geometry API. New source and Spec
lint is clean after correcting two test loop braces. Only self-review was used.

Residual limits: finite observer depth precision, transparent foreground contract,
browser video decoding and CORS. P3 qualifies sample assembly, tiled sources and
bounded upload / capture cost; owner visual acceptance remains pending.
